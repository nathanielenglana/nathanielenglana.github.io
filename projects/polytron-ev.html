Polytron, EV division. Feb 2025 to Feb 2026

Launch campaigns at Polytron EV

I was a social media specialist intern on the EV marketing team during the launch of the Polytron G3, a new electric car. My supervisor covered both the two-wheel and four-wheel lines, so I took on more of the day-to-day work, including campaigns for the electric two-wheelers.

Campaigns

Explaining EVA, the G3's roadside assistance

EVA is the emergency vehicle assistance that comes with the G3. I made a series of Reels explaining it, and did the concept, script, copy, shooting and editing myself.

For one of the Reels I suggested interviewing the G3 product manager on camera. That Reel got a clearly higher like rate than the one without the interview.

Ride to Way Kambas, Fox 350

A ride to Way Kambas on the Fox 350 electric motorcycle, filmed for YouTube. I planned the route, chose the KOLs and the crew, and designed a giveaway that got people to watch the film.

Kilometer Kebaikan 2026

Kilometer Kebaikan turns the kilometres that owners ride and drive into a donation. I set up the kilometre tracking with the IT team, added the G3 to the program, and did the outreach to Happy Hearts Indonesia for a school in NTT.

G3 Experience challenge

My concept: test drive the G3, post about it, and win a Polytron TV. People who joined posted their own content about the drive. I filmed and edited the winner's interview at GIIAS 2025.

Day to day

  • KOL partnerships, creative agency coordination and account management.
  • Weekly and monthly performance reports across Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube.
  • Picking the organic posts to put paid budget behind.
